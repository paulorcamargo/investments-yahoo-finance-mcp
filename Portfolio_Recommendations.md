## 4. Top Emerging Market ETFs for Canadians
To fill out your Emerging Markets allocation, consider these CAD-listed ETFs:

1. **XEC.TO (iShares Core MSCI Emerging Markets IMI Index ETF)**
   * **Pros:** Industry standard MSCI index. Includes tech-heavy markets like South Korea and Taiwan alongside China and India.
2. **VEE.TO (Vanguard FTSE Emerging Markets All Cap Index ETF)**
   * **Pros:** Lower management fees. Tracks FTSE (excludes South Korea, meaning heavier weighting toward China/India/Brazil).
3. **ZEM.TO (BMO MSCI Emerging Markets Index ETF)**
   * **Pros:** Holds international stocks directly rather than via a US wrapper, making it slightly more tax-efficient in certain Canadian accounts.
